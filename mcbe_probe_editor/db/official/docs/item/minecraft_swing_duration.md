# `minecraft:swing_duration`

- 出现版本：`1.26.0`
- 核实状态：**已核实**

## 说明

Duration, in seconds, of the item's swing animation played when mining or attacking. Affects visuals only and does not impact attack frequency or other gameplay mechanics.
