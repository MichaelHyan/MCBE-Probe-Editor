# `minecraft:body_rotation_always_follows_head`

- 出现版本：`1.21.0`、`1.26.0`
- 核实状态：**已核实**

## 说明

Causes the entity's body rotation to match the one of their head.Does not override the "minecraft:body_rotation_blocked" component.
